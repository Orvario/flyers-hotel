About Flyers Airport Hotel

Flyers Hotel − a great place to start and finish your journey

Flyers Airport Hotel is located in the heart of the Reykjanesbaer, just minutes from Keflavík International Airport. We offer clean and comfortable rooms for travellers arriving in or departing from Iceland. With 24/7 self check-in, you can check in at a time that suits you – whether you arrive late at night or leave early in the morning.

We focus on simplicity, cleanliness, and comfort, ensuring you can enjoy a restful stay before continuing your journey. The hotel is within walking distance to down town, pubs, restaurants and more. If you like a convenient and affordable place to stay. Flyers Airport Hotel got you covered.

Flyers Airport Hotel offers 19 spacious and comfortable rooms to suit all travellers.
The hotel features rooms with private and shared bathrooms as well as: 

Family rooms – spacious and perfect for the whole family

Double rooms – ideal for friends or couples

Single rooms – comfortable and quiet for solo travellers

All rooms come with freshly made beds, TV, cable and free WiFi as well as access to a shared lounge where guests can relax, socialise, or get some work done.
