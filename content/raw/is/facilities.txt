Facilities

At Flyers Airport Hotel, we focus on comfort, cleanliness, and simplicity. The hotel offers 19 well-equipped rooms that are regularly cleaned and well maintained.

All rooms feature freshly made beds with soft linens. A flat-screen TV with over 100 channels to choose from. Access to a shared lounge with comfortable seating. We offer family rooms, double rooms, and single rooms, so whether you’re travelling with family, a partner, or solo, you’ll find the perfect space to rest and relax. As an added bonus we are well situated in close proximity to restaurants, coffee shops, and bars.

We offer a good selection of Grab&Go food and beverage selection. Coffee, drinks, snacks, breakfast items and more. (From 01.02.2026)

03
restaurant and bars

Flyers Hotel is located in the center of town, walking distance to restaurants, bars, convenient store and shops. 

04
Travel around Reykjanes

Reykjanes peninsula is an active volcanic GeoPark. One of the wonders of Iceland. Reykjanes volcanic eruptions are frequent and to date about 12 eruptions have been in the area for the past four years. 

05
Outdoor community pool

Icelanders love their hot water pools and the pool center is only about 5 min. walking distance from the hotel. Enjoy the pool, suna, steam room, indoor playing pool for kids and more. 

06
Best Schnitzel in Iceland!

The polish food restaurant is situated next door to the Flyers Hotel offering lunch and homey atmosphere. You can get the best cowboy schnitzel in Iceland and flush in down with some Icelandic beer. 

07
Stay near the Airport

The trip to the capital is about 50 min. drive from the airport. We do love Reykjavik but it’s much more convenient to start your journey or finish a great trip  in our cosy town. Come stay at Flyers Hotel and make the most of it. 

08
Parking

Parking is easy at Flyers Hotel. We usually have ample parking available so you can keep your rental car here during your stay. No worries!
