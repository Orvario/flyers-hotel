Flyers Keflavík Airport Hotel

Located only a few minutes from Keflavík International Airport, Flyer Airport Hotel offers bright, clean, and comfortable rooms for every type of traveller. Choose from private or shared bathrooms, family rooms, doubles, triples, or singles – all with freshly made beds, TV, reading lights, wash basin, towels, and free high-speed Wi-Fi. With 24/7 self check-in and a perfect location for both early departures and late arrivals, our hotel is the ideal base for exploring the Reykjanes Peninsula or resting before your next journey.
